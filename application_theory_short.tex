\subsection{Randomized categorical views}
\label{sec:randomized_categorical_application}
We maintain a cross-product of reusable attribute reports for Covertype's
40 soil categories and 100-meter elevation bins in $[0,4000)$ ($n=40$).
Each arriving record supplies one paired report; the aggregate is retained
across rounds.
Symmetric unary encoding (SUE) reports a true bit with probability
$p_j=1-\eta_j$ and a false bit with probability
$\beta_j=\eta_j\in\{1/4,1/3\}$.
For a fresh-work instance, all report coins are independent and sampled
after program fixation. Conditioning on arbitrary source records and
history leaves coordinate atoms at most $1-\eta_j$, hence
$\lambda_U=\lambda_V=\eta_j$. Each attribute has privacy budget
$\varepsilon_j=2\log((1-\eta_j)/\eta_j)$; a paired report has
$2\varepsilon_j$. Privacy-matched optimized unary encoding (OUE) uses
$p_j=1/2$ and $\beta_j\in\{1/10,1/5\}$~\cite{wang2017local},
giving escape $\lambda_U=\lambda_V=\beta_j$.

For a prime $q>2$, set $r=2$, $B=I$, and retain one modular state $C$, where $I$ is the
identity matrix, $\mathbf1_r$ and $\mathbf1$ are all-ones vectors of length
$r$ and of the ambient dimension, and $\operatorname{lift}_q$ maps a field
element to its least nonnegative integer representative. The verifier
computes $m_U=U_j\mathbf1_r$ and $m_V=V_j\mathbf1_r$ from received
reports. Correct increments lie in $[0,2]$, so their unique lift,
the decoding increment
$\Gamma_j=\operatorname{lift}_q(\widetilde C_j-\widehat C_{j-1})$,
recovers $U_jV_j^\top$ despite cumulative wrap. An increment entry outside
$\{0,1,2\}$ causes rejection without state advance. On acceptance, advance
$C$ and accumulate outside the field:
\[
 \widehat H=\sum_j
 \frac{\Gamma_j-\beta_jm_U\mathbf1^\top-\beta_j\mathbf1m_V^\top
                   +r\beta_j^2\mathbf1\mathbf1^\top}{(p_j-\beta_j)^2}.
\]
Correct maintenance gives an unbiased estimator $\widehat H$ of the joint
category counts by independence of the two report noises. The uniform-factor work profile is unnecessary
for this nonuniform application.

Alternate the two parameter types over 512 rounds. Under the global
budget $W\le128n^2$, $R\ge n^2$ permits the conservative allowance
$b=128$ in Theorem~\ref{thm:fresh-stateful}, using the largest
384 acceptance coefficients. Sorting all 512 rounds gives the global
work-certificate floors
\[
 f_{\rm SUE}=(625/1024)^{64},\qquad
 f_{\rm OUE}=\bigl((99/100)^4(24/25)^2\bigr)^{64}.
\]
SUE can certify the target $(31/50)^{64}$; OUE cannot reach it through
this threshold certificate.

For the two alternating types, the exact allocator admits a faster
specialization of~\eqref{ext:eq-hetero-objective}. Let $m=256$, $b=128$,
integer $T\ge2m$, $S=T-2m$, and give $S_A$ extra checks to
the $\eta=1/4$ type and $S-S_A$ to the $\eta=1/3$ type.
Convexity and permutation symmetry within each type, as in
Theorem~\ref{ext:thm-balanced}, ensure a within-type balanced optimum.
Because $15/16>8/9+(1/9)q^{-1}$ for $q\ge17$, the objective always
retains all $m$ first-type coefficients and the largest $m-b$ second-type
coefficients. Its logarithm is affine between the integer breakpoints
\[
 \{0,S\}\cup\bigl(\{hm,\ S-hm,\ S-hm-b:h\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}\}\cap[0,S]\bigr).
\]
At a multiple of $m$ a balanced level changes; the shifted breakpoints
occur when the $b$ excluded second-type entries cross a level.
Evaluating these endpoints with exact rational products therefore finds
the global optimum at fixed $T$. Monotonicity in $T$ then permits a
search for the smallest total meeting the target, verified against the
optimum at the preceding integer total.

Section~\ref{sec:connected-results} combines the work and integrity
requirements at $q=65521$; the $q=17$ configuration isolates work-target
allocation. Estimation error remains a separate statistical quantity. The implemented fixed bilinear evaluator uses trusted
kernel admission and input disclosure; its gate count is a logical
resource measure, not hostile-host attestation.
