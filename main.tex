\section{Discussion and Conclusion}\label{sec:scope}\label{sec:conclusion}
Retained products and arbitrary paid history can coexist with a positive
fresh-work guarantee. Rank conditioning and resource recursion give
work-dependent acceptance bounds, while exact allocation converts a target
into a finite checking decision. Exact-field measurements quantify reuse
benefits, and randomized Covertype views instantiate the nonuniform case.
The guarantees apply to the stated causal ordering and bilinear class;
untrusted general-purpose execution still needs an enforcement mechanism.
The empirical results also identify where reuse stops paying: setup costs,
candidate freezing, and checking remain visible even when the incremental
kernel is small.
